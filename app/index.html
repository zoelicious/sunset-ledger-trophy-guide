Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 — long, not hard. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so never use them on your trophy save. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so the saves in Phase 0 are your only safety net.

    Before you go on a rampage: your Criminal Profile can reach “Irredeemable”, and it is permanent. Do the wanton-destruction moments deliberately, not by accident, early in the game. Source: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap; GTABase via eGamers.

    Sources & honesty

    Every guide in this ledger is written in our own words. A source is cited, never copied. The official trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them — that is the one exception, and it is why the list matches the official 55/55.

    What we do not know yet

    • 5 trophies have unconfirmed triggers — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin. Their guides say only what the official description gives, and carry the label “Trigger not yet confirmed”. No unlock steps are invented for them.
    • The Chapter 1 meditation spot is described two ways — PowerPyx says “at Jason's house”, OutputLag and eGamers say Lucia's balcony. This guide does not pick one: it says “one early meditation spot in Chapter 1” and cites both.
    • 4 or 5 chapters? The trophy list names Chapters 1–4 plus a final mission; PowerPyx describes the story as 5 chapters. This guide does not claim a chapter count anywhere.
    • 28 of the 55 trophies are hidden on PSN until earned. This guide hides the 12 story trophies behind the spoiler setting until you earn them.
