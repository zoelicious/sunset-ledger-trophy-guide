Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 — long, not hard. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so never use them on your trophy save. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so the saves in Phase 0 are your only safety net.

    Before you go on a rampage: your Criminal Profile can reach “Irredeemable”, and it is permanent. Do the wanton-destruction moments deliberately, not by accident, early in the game. Source: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap; GTABase via eGamers.

    Missables — 3 with a point of no return

    From the official descriptions + cited launch guides. Positive Mental Attitude is the one trophy every guide flags as missable. The other two are flagged time-sensitive by launch guides — play them early rather than testing the cutoff. Our own research only strongly supports Positive Mental Attitude as missable; the time-sensitive flags below come from the launch guides cited on each card.

    Ch 1 · Positive Mental Attitude Ch 3 · Strangers & Freaks Mid-story · A Long Road

    ⚠ MissableBronze · #28

    Positive Mental Attitude

    Point of no return: the end of Chapter 1 — one early meditation spot is gone once that chapter ends, and there is no mission replay or New Game+ to go back.

    Meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida's 10 locations. Meditate at every spot the moment you see one, and make a manual save at the start of each chapter. Sources disagree on the exact Chapter 1 spot — PowerPyx places it at Jason's house, OutputLag and eGamers at Lucia's balcony — so treat any early meditation prompt in Chapter 1 as the one that matters.

    Sources: PowerPyx · OutputLag · eGamers — retrieved 2026-11-19

    Time-sensitiveSilver · #25

    Strangers & Freaks

    Point of no return: Chapter 3 — launch guides flag the “Race Against Yago” strand as time-sensitive, with its cutoff in Chapter 3.

    Complete 8 mission strands for strangers across Leonida. Finish stranger strands as they appear instead of leaving them for post-story cleanup — the Chapter 3 strand is the one the guides warn about. The counter in the tracker shows 0/8.

    Sources: PowerPyx · OutputLag · KeenGamer — retrieved 2026-11-19

    Time-sensitiveGold · #14

    A Long Road

    Point of no return: mid-story — some optional missions that feed the 100% completion checklist close before the finale, so a checklist left for post-story cleanup may already be locked out.

    Attain 100% Completion. Do optional missions as they appear and keep a manual save from mid-story, so a closed-off mission cannot strand the checklist. The bar itself is forgiving — about 60% true completion by PowerPyx's count — but only if nothing it needs has already closed.

    Sources: PowerPyx · OutputLag · KeenGamer — retrieved 2026-11-19

    No mission replay, no New Game+. Once a chapter ends you cannot replay its missions. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one more before the final mission — those saves are your only safety net. Source: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap; OutputLag; eGamers.
    Cheats disable trophies and saving. Never use cheats on your trophy save — they switch trophies off. PowerPyx lists 61 cheats, all unlocked only after the credits; keep them off this save entirely. Source: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap.

    Sources & honesty

    Every guide in this ledger is written in our own words. A source is cited, never copied. The official trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them — that is the one exception, and it is why the list matches the official 55/55.

    What we do not know yet

    • 5 trophies have unconfirmed triggers — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin. Their guides say only what the official description gives, and carry the label “Trigger not yet confirmed”. No unlock steps are invented for them.
    • The Chapter 1 meditation spot is described two ways — PowerPyx says “at Jason's house”, OutputLag and eGamers say Lucia's balcony. This guide does not pick one: it says “one early meditation spot in Chapter 1” and cites both.
    • 4 or 5 chapters? The trophy list names Chapters 1–4 plus a final mission; PowerPyx describes the story as 5 chapters. This guide does not claim a chapter count anywhere.
    • 28 of the 55 trophies are hidden on PSN until earned. This guide hides the 12 story trophies behind the spoiler setting until you earn them.
